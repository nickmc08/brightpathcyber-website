### Idea A: Civic Modernism
**Design Movement:** Mid-century American institutional modernism — think the clean authority of a well-funded nonprofit or regional bank, updated for 2026.
**Core Principles:**
1. Structural clarity — every element earns its place through function
2. Authoritative restraint — confidence without ostentation
3. Human warmth — professional but never cold
4. Layered hierarchy — clear visual pathways guide the reader

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0F2044) as the bedrock of authority. Warm white (#FAF9F6) backgrounds that feel like quality paper, not a screen. Teal (#1B9E8E) as a signal of clarity and forward motion — used sparingly as accent. Amber (#D4882A) for the youth division only, creating instant brand separation.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial columns — hero sections use a 60/40 split rather than centered stacks. Section dividers use subtle diagonal rules. Content blocks alternate between left-anchored and right-anchored to create rhythm without chaos.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rules in teal that "underline" section headers
- Large numeral callouts (01, 02, 03) for service ladders and steps
- Generous left-margin white space that frames content like a book

**Interaction Philosophy:** Deliberate and calm. Hover states reveal depth (subtle shadow lift on cards). Scroll-triggered fade-ins that feel like turning a page, not a pop-up.

**Animation:** Slow, confident entrance animations (0.6s ease-out). Cards lift 4px on hover with a shadow bloom. No bouncing or spinning — this is a consulting firm, not a startup.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (serif) — authority and tradition for headings
- Body: Source Sans 3 — highly readable, warm, professional
- Accent labels: Montserrat Semibold — clean caps for division names and CTAs

### Idea B: Pacific Northwest Professional (CHOSEN)
**Design Movement:** Contemporary Pacific Northwest corporate identity — clean lines, natural warmth, and the quiet confidence of a company that knows its community.
**Core Principles:**
1. Grounded authority — deep charcoal and navy anchor every page
2. Approachable warmth — warm whites and soft teal prevent sterility
3. Division clarity — ClearPath Cyber and Launchpad Money each have distinct visual identities within the parent brand
4. Editorial precision — typography-led design with intentional hierarchy

**Color Philosophy:** Charcoal-navy (#1A2744) as primary — more approachable than pure black, more authoritative than medium blue. Warm white (#F8F7F4) backgrounds. Teal (#0D9488) as the primary accent — signals clarity, trust, and forward movement. ClearPath Cyber uses cool blue-teal tones; Launchpad Money uses energetic amber-gold (#F59E0B) to signal energy and optimism.

**Layout Paradigm:** Left-anchored editorial with intentional asymmetry. Hero sections use large typographic statements left-aligned with supporting imagery right. Service sections use a 3-column grid that breaks into featured cards. The parent brand sits above both divisions with a clear visual hierarchy.

**Signature Elements:**
- Diagonal accent bars in teal that appear at section transitions
- Division "badges" — small pill labels that identify which brand content belongs to
- Large, confident display type that commands attention without shouting

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth and purposeful. Navigation highlights the active section. Cards have a clean lift effect. CTAs pulse subtly to draw attention without being aggressive.

**Animation:** Entrance animations tied to scroll position — content slides in from slightly below (24px) with opacity fade. Duration 0.5s with ease-out. Stagger children by 0.1s for lists. Hover states: 3px lift + shadow deepening on cards.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Fraunces (variable serif) — distinctive, warm, authoritative for hero text
- Body: DM Sans — modern, highly legible, friendly
- Labels/CTAs: DM Sans Semibold — consistent with body but clearly differentiated

### Idea C: Structured Clarity
**Design Movement:** Swiss International Style meets American corporate warmth — grid-based precision with human touches.
**Core Principles:**
1. Grid discipline — 12-column underlying structure visible in every layout decision
2. Color as communication — each division's palette is a semantic signal
3. Typography as architecture — type size and weight carry the entire visual hierarchy
4. Negative space as luxury — generous padding signals quality

**Color Philosophy:** Pure navy (#0B1F3A) for maximum authority. Cream (#FDF8F0) for warmth. Teal (#14B8A6) for McMillon Co. brand. Each division gets a secondary accent: ClearPath uses steel blue, Launchpad uses electric yellow-green.

**Layout Paradigm:** Strict grid with deliberate breaks — hero sections span full width, content sections snap to the 12-column grid. Tables and comparison layouts are used for service ladders. Clean, predictable, scannable.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin 1px borders in teal that frame content blocks
- Monospaced type for statistics and data points
- Icon-forward service cards with consistent visual language

**Interaction Philosophy:** Functional and efficient. Hover states are immediate and clear. No decorative animations — only purposeful transitions that aid navigation.

**Animation:** Minimal — 0.2s opacity transitions only. Content appears cleanly without theatrical entrance effects. Focus is on content, not motion.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Sora — geometric, modern, clean
- Body: Nunito Sans — friendly and readable
- Data/Stats: JetBrains Mono — technical credibility for numbers

## Selected Direction: **Idea B — Pacific Northwest Professional**

This direction best serves McMillon Co.'s dual mandate: professional credibility for the parent brand, and distinct visual identities for both divisions. The Fraunces/DM Sans pairing gives warmth without sacrificing authority. The teal accent system is flexible enough to work across both divisions while the amber accent for Launchpad Money creates immediate visual differentiation.
